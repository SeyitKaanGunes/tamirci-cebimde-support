How Fixy works

  1. Choose Apprentice or Master mode on the home screen.
  2. Pick the repair category, add a photo and/or describe the problem in a few sentences.
  3. Fixy shows the likely cause, the risk level, safety warnings, materials and a step-by-step plan.

Fixy has no ads and no credits. There is free use and an optional Premium subscription.

How subscriptions work

  • Premium is offered as weekly, monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions. The current price, period and any free trial are shown in your local currency in the app before you buy.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; the renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the period ends.
  • If a free trial is offered, the paid period starts unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you buy another plan.

Restoring purchases

If you moved to a new iPhone or Premium does not show: open the Premium screen in Fixy and tap Restore. You must be signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy. Restoring can take a few seconds; then close and reopen the app.

Cancelling or changing your subscription

Apple manages subscriptions; deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone and tap your name at the top.
  2. Tap Subscriptions > Fixy.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription, or choose another plan.

Shortcut: apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. After cancelling, Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Apple handles refund requests: reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Fixy has no accounts; it works with a random ID created on your device. You can delete your server data in the app:

  1. Tap the gear icon on the home screen to open Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your history, saved guides, preferences, feedback, usage counters, this device's own subscription events and the subscription copy on our server. Only the daily limit counters expire on their own within 7 days, so that deleting data cannot reset a daily limit: today's counter of this device, and the subscription's daily counters, which are kept under a one-way code of the store subscription. If your subscription moved between devices of the same Apple ID, the link between those two device IDs is kept so the other device keeps Premium; it is deleted automatically 30 days after the subscription ends. It does not cancel your subscription (use the steps above). The random device ID stays on your device so that purchases can be restored; if you have an active subscription, your Premium status comes back at the next sync. Uninstalling the app does not delete your data on our server; use the steps above. If you cannot use the app, email us; we reply within 30 days. Details: Privacy Policy.

Where do my photos and descriptions go?

They are sent through our server to AI providers (primarily Anthropic's Claude models) for analysis. Photos are not stored on our servers. Descriptions and answers are kept until you delete them, for at most 12 months, so that you can see them in History. We never use data for advertising and never sell it. Full list: Privacy Policy.

Safety

Fixy is guidance; it does not replace a qualified professional or emergency services. For a gas smell, a carbon monoxide alarm, a burning smell, sparks, smoke, electricity in contact with water or structural damage, Fixy shows a red warning at the top of the analysis screen: stop the work, move away from the area and contact a professional. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Common situations

I see “daily limit reached”

Free use has a daily analysis limit that resets the next day. On a shared network (mobile data, office or school Wi-Fi) free analyses can also pause for the rest of the day once that network has made a lot of them; the message then names the network. Premium allows many more analyses a day, under fair use: a daily ceiling per subscription against abuse (up to 100 text and 40 photo analyses, shared by all devices that restored the subscription) and per-minute limits. When it is reached, the message says so and the limit resets the next day (UTC).

I see “the service is busy”

Fixy’s AI is very busy. Try again later; at very busy times free analyses can stay paused until the next day (UTC). Danger alerts still work, and nothing is lost.

The analysis does not start

Fixy asks for data processing consent before the first analysis. If you turned it off, turn it on under Settings > AI data consent (switch Allow AI analysis). Also check your internet connection.

I bought Premium but it does not show

Restore your purchases. If that does not help, email us.
