Fixy Privacy Policy

Türkçe sürüm

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to the Fixy iOS app (version 1.2.0 and later) and its server.

At a glance
  • Fixy has no accounts. There are no ads, no tracking for advertising and we never sell your data.
  • Your description and photo are sent through our server to AI providers (primarily Anthropic's Claude models) to create your repair guide. Photos are not stored on our servers.
  • Your history (for up to 12 months) and saved guides are stored on our server, linked to a random ID created on your device, until you delete them. Saved guides of a device that has not used the app for 12 months are deleted.
  • You can delete your server data at any time in the app: Settings > Delete my data.
  • Apple takes subscription payments and RevenueCat manages subscription status. We never see your card details.

1. Who is responsible and how to reach us

Fixy is developed and provided by the independent developer Seyit Kaan Güneş (Türkiye), who is the data controller under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). For any privacy question or request: seyitkaangunes@icloud.com

3. Why we process data and on what legal basis

  • Creating the analysis and guide you ask for, and providing History and Saved guides: performance of a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c; GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Sending your description and photos to AI providers and processing them abroad: your explicit consent, requested in the app before your first analysis (KVKK Art. 5/1 and Art. 9; GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Verifying your subscription and unlocking Premium: performance of a contract.
  • Security, usage limits, preventing abuse and fraud: legitimate interests (KVKK Art. 5/2-f; GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Legal obligations and requests from authorities: KVKK Art. 5/2-ç; GDPR Art. 6(1)(c).

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or sale. The app contains no ads and no advertising SDK.

5. International transfers

Our servers and providers are located outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area, mainly in the USA and Singapore. These transfers rely on the explicit consent you give before your first analysis (KVKK Art. 9) and on appropriate safeguards such as the standard contractual clauses offered by the providers (GDPR Art. 46). If you withdraw your consent, no new analysis can be made.

7. Your choices and rights

In the app

  • Delete my data: open Settings with the gear icon on the home screen and choose Delete my data. It deletes your history, saved guides, preferences, feedback, usage counters (the daily limit counters expire on their own within 7 days), your own subscription events and the subscription copy from our server. A subscription transfer link between two of your device IDs, if one exists, is kept until 30 days after the subscription ends (see section 6). This cannot be undone. It does not cancel your subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings (how to). The random device ID stays on your device so that your purchases can be restored.
  • AI data consent: you can withdraw your consent under Settings > AI data consent (switch Allow AI analysis). No analysis can be made while it is off. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.
  • Delete single items: you can delete saved guides one by one.
  • Access: the History and Saved guides screens show the content we keep for you on our server.

Your legal rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, request information, learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly, know the third parties it is transferred to, request correction, deletion or destruction, ask that these actions are notified to recipients, object to a result against you produced solely by automated systems, and claim compensation. If you are in the EU/EEA, the GDPR gives you the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent.

Send requests to seyitkaangunes@icloud.com; we reply within 30 days. Because there are no accounts, we can find your records only through the random ID in the app, so the fastest way to delete them is Delete my data in the app. You can also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or to the data protection authority of your EU country.

8. Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS (TLS). AI and payment keys are kept only on the server, database access is restricted and we process only the data the service needs. No method is 100% secure, but we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

9. Children

Fixy is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If the age of digital consent is higher in your country, use the app with a parent's permission. Work involving electricity, gas or heights is an adult responsibility. If we learn that a child has sent us data, we delete it.
